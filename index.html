FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Do you need water or electricity from my house/building?

No! Our mobile car wash vans are 100% self-sufficient. We carry our own high-capacity water tanks, silent power generators, and professional pressure washers. You don't have to provide anything.

What is the difference between Normal Wash and VIP Wash?

Normal Wash (40 AED / 50 AED) covers full exterior foam wash, interior vacuuming, dashboard wipe, tire dressing and glass polish.

VIP Wash (60 AED / 80 AED) includes all normal wash services PLUS deep seat & carpet shampoo stain extraction, leather conditioning & UV protection, high-gloss tire sealant, and AC vent sanitization.

Which areas in Abu Dhabi do you service?

We cover ALL of Abu Dhabi: Al Reem Island, Saadiyat, Yas Island, Khalifa City, MBZ City, Al Raha, Corniche, Al Bateen, Al Khalidiyah, Al Mushrif, Mussafah, Shakhbout City, and all surrounding areas.

How quickly can you arrive after I book?

We offer on-demand doorstep service usually arriving within 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic and your specific area in Abu Dhabi. You can also schedule a specific date and time slot in advance!

How do I pay?

You can pay comfortably after inspecting your sparkling clean car using Cash, Card, or direct instant bank transfer.
