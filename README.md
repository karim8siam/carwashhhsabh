# DAYLAN Frank Car Washing Abu Dhabi — Mobile Doorstep Detailing Website

A high-converting, responsive, luxury automotive landing page and booking application created for **DAYLAN Frank Car Washing** (Abu Dhabi, UAE), inspired by top UAE automotive detailing brands with enhanced conversion funnels, instant WhatsApp dispatch, and live price calculators.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Innovations

1. **Exact Official Pricing & Packages Integration**:
   - **Normal Wash**: 
     - Sedan / Salon (Voitures Classiques): **40 AED**
     - SUV / Big (Grands Véhicules): **50 AED**
   - **VIP Wash (Detailing & Seats/Carpet Clean)**:
     - VIP Salon: **60 AED**
     - VIP Big (SUV): **80 AED**
2. **Instant 1-Click WhatsApp Booking Generator**:
   - Pre-formats the customer's selected vehicle, package, price, and Abu Dhabi location directly into a WhatsApp chat dispatched to `+971 55 643 9886` and `+971 52 122 4205`.
3. **Interactive Before & After Detailing Slider**:
   - Draggable comparison slider showing dust/sand removal vs showroom gloss finish.
4. **All Abu Dhabi Doorstep Coverage Map**:
   - Al Reem Island, Saadiyat, Yas Island, Khalifa City, MBZ City, Al Raha Beach, Corniche, Al Bateen, Al Khalidiyah, Mussafah, etc.
5. **100% Self-Equipped Vans Showcase**:
   - Highlights that no customer water or electricity hookup is needed.
6. **Mobile-First Luxury Dark Theme**:
   - Navy blue, gold yellow (`#FFB703`), carbon textures, glowing glassmorphism, sticky WhatsApp & Call action buttons.
7. **Original Promo Flyer Modal**:
   - Integrated promo flyer viewer showing the original poster and contact card.
